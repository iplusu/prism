\section*{Protocol v1: inference, simulation, and replay}
Define $B=1-(C/D)_{\rm targeted}/(C/D)_{\rm SFT}$, where $C/D$ is acquisition cost per accepted detection. The primary population is the oracle-supported constructed cohort.

After unsealing, score only saved predictions under the frozen artifacts and rules. All three paired seeds in both arms must qualify for a primary effect; otherwise report attained metrics, costs, and non-qualification without dropping seeds or substituting zeros. Average each arm's three first-qualifying $C/D$ values arithmetically. Paired audit-cluster bootstrap replicates retain seeds together and recompute matching and the entire first-crossing search, then form the arm-mean ratio for $B$. Efficacy requires original-sample qualification and at least 95\% qualifying replicates. Report conditional intervals, undefined-denominator/non-qualification frequencies, and seed variability; uncertainty conditions on the acquired corpus. Common-recall fallback is descriptive.

\textbf{Qualification/power plan.} Before M3, run 1,000 cluster-level simulations per scenario: 10/20/30\% witness yield, intracluster correlations 0/0.1/0.3, and true $B=0,0.20,0.30$, with 350 executable confirmatory attempts in 35 planned clusters (35/70/105 witnesses before attrition, not observations). Exclude the three static-only CWEs from the outset. Explicitly recompute qualification probability, conditional power, unconditional efficacy-claim probability, and expected 95\% interval widths for $B$ after applying each scenario's buildability, witness-acceptance, unresolved-case, and M4 class exclusions; simulate retained cluster sizes rather than retaining the nominal 35 clusters. Repeat with M1 estimates and any M3 allocation reductions. Simulate checkpoints and three paired seeds with shared labels/costs and 2,000 paired bootstrap replicates. Target 80\% all-six qualification and 80\% conditional probability of $L>0.20$ at $B=0.30$; the implied unconditional efficacy-claim target is $0.80\times0.80=0.64$ (approximately 64\%), not achieved power. Report simulated mean and median widths among qualifying replicates, non-qualification frequencies, and false useful-benefit claims at $B\leq0.20$; actual post-exclusion power and widths remain to be computed because exclusion rates and the joint checkpoint/cost distribution are unmeasured. Use M1 estimates to freeze a positive-bearing-cluster minimum at M3; revise infeasible scope or designate feasibility-only. No achieved power is claimed.

Capacity includes prediction generation and storage for all 66 checkpoints on both 500-attempt audit subsets (66,000 candidate-checkpoint scoring passes before exclusions), plus post-unsealing scoring of saved confirmatory predictions and the planned bootstrap workload.


\textbf{Validation prerequisite and fallback.} Before M3, validate the entire checkpoint-selection and interval procedure against the prespecified simulation grid. Require a false useful-benefit claim rate no greater than 5\% at $B\leq0.20$ and at least 95\% conditional interval coverage in qualifying simulations. If the first-crossing rule fails, prespecify comparison at the final scheduled checkpoint under the common caps, retaining the same qualification floors, all three paired seeds, and cluster bootstrap. Validate this fixed-checkpoint procedure against the same criteria before confirmatory inference; if it also fails, report descriptive results only. Freeze the selected rule before confirmatory labels or scores are accessed.

\textbf{Replay.} M3 freezes training replay sampling, batch size, steps, and unresolved-slot handling. Paired seeds use matching replay random streams where applicable. Harness acceptance requires two scheduled replays under declared policies and pinned fixtures. Reproduction is the fraction of accepted witnesses for which both replays reproduce the policy violation, with the M4 threshold $\lceil0.95n\rceil$ of $n$ accepted witnesses. Log review, repair, replay labor and compute, including failed attempts and retries; cached evidence is not recharged.
