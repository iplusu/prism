\begin{abstract}
We study an adaptive step-size rule for proximal gradient methods applied to
sparse regression. The rule estimates local curvature from consecutive
gradients and requires no line search. On three benchmark datasets it reduces
the number of iterations by 31\% on average compared with a fixed step size.
\end{abstract}
