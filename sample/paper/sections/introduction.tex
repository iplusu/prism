\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

Sparse regression problems of the form
\begin{equation}
  \min_{x \in \mathbb{R}^n} \; \frac{1}{2}\lVert Ax - b \rVert_2^2 + \lambda \lVert x \rVert_1
  \label{eq:lasso}
\end{equation}
are a standard tool in statistics and signal processing~\citep{tibshirani1996}.
Proximal gradient methods solve \eqref{eq:lasso} efficiently when the step size
is chosen well~\citep{beck2009}. A fixed step size $1/L$ requires the Lipschitz
constant $L$ of the gradient, which is often unknown or pessimistic.

Our contributions are an adaptive rule that estimates curvature from
consecutive gradients (\cref{sec:method}) and an empirical comparison on three
datasets (\cref{sec:results}).
